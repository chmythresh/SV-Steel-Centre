Our Heritage

Serving Vijayawada’s Industrial Backbone Since 1989

Founded in 1989, Sri Venkateswara Steel Centre (SV Steel Centre) started with a clear industrial objective: to give coastal Andhra Pradesh manufacturers seamless, fair, and reliable access to flat steel products without prohibitive minimum order quantities.

Situated at the epicentre of Vijayawada's commercial iron trade in Bhavanipuram, we have witnessed the city's transformation from regional market town to a bustling commercial capital. Throughout, our commitment has remained rock-solid: providing solely genuine primary-grade Cold Rolled (CR) and Zinc / Galvanized (GI) sheets.

Today, our experienced team provides technical guidance on sheet thickness gauges, zinc GSM coatings, and theoretical-to-actual weight calculations to hundreds of daily fabricators, agricultural builders, switchgear engineers, and government contractors.
