Theoretical Weight vs. Actual Scale Weight

The calculator applies the standard IS nominal density of 7,850 kg/m³. Due to metallurgical rolling mill tolerances (±0.02mm to ±0.05mm), the actual weighbridge ticket may display a minor variation of ±1% to 2%. Commercial invoicing is consistently based on certified gross-minus-tare scales.

Zinc Coating Weight (GSM) Calculation

For Galvanized Iron (GI), zinc coatings add mass according to grams per square meter (e.g., 90 GSM, 120 GSM, 275 GSM). Our GI selector uses a density coefficient of 7,870 kg/m³ to account for prime zinc immersion on both surfaces per IS 277 specifications.

Standard IS 513 CR Grades Explained

CRCA (Cold Rolled Close Annealed) sheets are available in Ordinary (CR1/O), Drawing (CR2/D), Deep Drawing (CR3/DD), and Extra Deep Drawing (CR4/EDD). Each grade exhibits differing elongation limits for bending, stamping, and press working.
